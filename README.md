## ✨ Key Features

- **📶 100% Offline Functionality:**
  - Works entirely without mobile data, Wi-Fi, or cellular network.
  - Zero cloud latency; instantaneous transaction logging and retrieval.
  - All data is saved directly on your local device storage.

- **💰 Money Tracking (Given & Taken):**
  - **Money Given (Gave):** Keep a clear record of money lent to friends, relatives, or customers.
  - **Money Taken (Got):** Track borrowing, supplier payments, and debts you need to clear.
  - **Automatic Balance Calculation:** Instantly calculates who owes whom and the total net outstanding balance.

- **🔒 Total Data Privacy:**
  - No account registration, OTP, or email sign-in required.
  - Your financial ledger remains strictly confidential on your personal phone.

- **⚡ Lightweight & Responsive:**
  - Optimized for low resource usage and smooth performance across entry-level and flagship Android devices alike.
